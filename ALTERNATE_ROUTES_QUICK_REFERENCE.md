# Alternate Routes - Quick Reference Card

## 🚀 At a Glance

Your Route Finder now shows **3-5 alternate routes** for every journey, automatically calculated and displayed on the map with different colors.

---

## 📍 How It Works

```
1. Enter start & end location
2. Click "Find Route"
3. System calculates all alternatives
4. Choose your preferred route
```

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Why 3-5 routes every time?**
A: Multiple options ensure you can choose based on your needs—speed, distance, traffic, or preference.

**Q: Can routes change?**
A: Yes! Traffic updates every 15 seconds, so routes may shift based on current conditions.

**Q: How long are calculations?**
A: Usually 2-5 seconds. Complex routes may take longer.

**Q: Can I edit routes?**
A: Not yet, but coming soon! For now, you can choose from available alternatives.

**Q: Do routes save?**
A: Currently only for this session. New search = new routes.
