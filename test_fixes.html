Traffic Color System:

The new traffic visualization system works as follows:

  • Blue (#0066ff): Free flowing traffic (traffic level < 0.33)
  • Orange (#ff8800): Moderate traffic (traffic level 0.33-0.66)
  • Red (#ff0000): Heavy traffic (traffic level > 0.66)

Traffic levels are simulated based on:

  • Route progress (higher traffic in middle sections representing city centers)
  • Route length (longer routes have more varied traffic)
  • Random factors for realistic variation
